Chalk theme

A plain, fast Shopify theme for fitness stores that sell supplements, apparel and gear in one place. This guide walks through every setting and section.

1.0.0Current version
OS 2.0Sections on every page
0 appsNeeded to run the theme

Getting started

  1. In your Shopify admin open Online Store > Themes and add Chalk from the Theme Store, or choose Add theme > Upload zip file if you have the file.
  2. Click Customize. The theme opens with the Chalk style and a ready home page.
  3. Set your logo, colors and font in Theme settings (the brush icon in the editor's left bar).
  4. Create the menus main-menu and footer in Content > Menus if your store does not have them yet. The header, phone menu and footer read them by default.
  5. Replace the demo photos and texts section by section, then preview on desktop and phone with the device switch at the top of the editor.
  6. When you are happy, click Publish.
Before you startDuplicate the theme (Actions > Duplicate) and keep the copy as a backup. Theme updates install as a new copy, so your published store never changes without you.

Theme settings

GroupWhat it controls
TypographyOne font for the whole store. Regular, medium, semibold, bold and italic are loaded automatically.
ColorsBackground and Text for every page. Secondary text uses the text color at lower opacity, so it always keeps readable contrast. Panel background fills the footer, menus and the cart. Accent and Text on accent style badges, sale prices, the logo dot and the Buy it now button.
CartFree shipping from: an amount in your store currency. The cart shows a progress bar toward it. Set 0 to hide the bar.
Social mediaLinks for Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, X, Pinterest, Threads, Snapchat, Tumblr, Vimeo and LinkedIn. Each filled field adds an icon to the footer. Leave a field empty to hide it.
FaviconThe small icon in the browser tab. Use a square image, 96 by 96 pixels or larger.

Header and menus

Announcement

A thin bar above the header with a left text, a countdown and a right text. Write the countdown end as 2026-12-31 23:59. When the date passes, the countdown hides itself.

Header

  • Logo: an image. Without one, the store name is set in the theme font with an accent dot.
  • Menu: the desktop menu, main-menu by default. A link that has child links opens the Shop panel.
  • Shop panel: up to three link columns (First column menu, Second column menu, Second column, lower menu), a few products and a promo photo with a label, heading and link.
  • Search: results appear while typing (products, pages, articles and suggestions).
  • Country and language: the picker shows on its own once you sell to more than one country or publish more than one language.

Phone and tablet menu

  • Main links: large links with the number of products in each collection. Defaults to main-menu.
  • Quick links: small chips under the main links, good for Best sellers or Sale.
  • Other links: plain links at the bottom, such as About or Contact. Defaults to footer.

Footer

A tagline with a line under it, two menu columns, a social column (from Theme settings > Social media), Follow on Shop, your store policies and the payment icons. The large wordmark at the bottom uses the store name.

Home page sections

Add, hide and reorder sections from the left bar of the editor. Every section below can go on any page that uses sections.

Product page

Photos sit on the left, the buy column on the right. On a phone the photos come first. The buy column is built from blocks you can reorder or remove:

Title, PriceThe title and the price with compare-at price, unit price, tax note and Shop Pay installments.
Lead lineOne sentence under the price, from the block or the custom.lead metafield.
Short factsSmall chips from the custom.chips metafield.
Variant pickerColor swatches and option buttons. Picking a variant shows its own photo first.
Buy buttonsQuantity, Add to cart and Buy it now, subscription options when the product has selling plans, the gift card recipient form, and pickup availability.
Trust linesUp to three short lines about shipping, returns and payment.
Collapsible rowA row that opens. Content comes from the product description, the How to use metafields or your own text.
Complementary productsProducts that go well together, set in the Search & Discovery app.
Apps, Custom LiquidBlocks from installed apps (reviews, subscriptions) and your own code.

Photos by color

Give each photo an alt text with the color name, exactly as in the Color option (for example Black). When a shopper picks that color, only its photos are shown. Photos without a color name show for every color. You can also attach one image to each variant in the product editor; the theme moves it to the front.

Size guide

Create a page with your size table, then pick it in Product > Size guide page and list the product types that need it (for example Apparel). A Size guide link appears next to the Size option and opens the page in a dialog.

Video and 3D

Videos, YouTube or Vimeo links and 3D models added to a product appear in the gallery. 3D models get the View in your space button on phones that support it.

Collections and search

  • Filters and sorting: install the free Shopify Search & Discovery app and choose filters there (availability, price, product type, options, metafields). The Filters button shows on collection and search pages.
  • Range chips: a row of chips at the top of every collection. Pick a menu in Catalog > Range chips. Its top links become the chips; child links become a second row.
  • Collection image and description: shown under the title. Turn the image off with Show collection image.
  • Promo tile: a photo card between products, on the collections you pick.
  • Products per page: from 12 to 48.

Cart

Add to cart opens the cart drawer on every page: lines with quantity, the free shipping bar and the subtotal. The View cart link opens the full cart page with the order note, discounts, taxes and express checkout buttons (Shop Pay, Apple Pay, Google Pay and other methods you turn on in Settings > Payments).

Hide the order note in Cart > Show order note.

Journal

The blog list shows cards with the photo, tag, reading time, title and a short excerpt (the article's excerpt, or the start of the text).

  • Card photo: article covers are wide, the cards are tall. Add a portrait image to the custom.card_image metafield and the card uses it, so people stay in the frame.
  • In short: points at the top of an article, one per line, from custom.in_short.
  • Products in this article from custom.products and Sources from custom.sources at the end of the article.
  • Comments: turn them on per blog in Content > Blog posts > Manage blogs.

About, contact, FAQ

  • About chapters: chapters with text on the left and a sticky photo on the right that follows the chapter you are reading.
  • Contact on a photo: the Shopify contact form drawn in white lines over a photo. Messages arrive at your store email.
  • FAQ: questions in tabs, with an aside block and a contact line at the bottom.

To use them, create a page and pick the template page.about, page.contact or page.faq in the page editor.

Gift cards

Create a gift card product in Products > Gift cards. On its product page, shoppers can send the card to someone else with a name, email, message and send date. The gift card page they receive shows the balance, the code with a copy button, a QR code for in-store use, Apple Wallet and a print button.

Languages and currencies

  • Currencies: add countries in Settings > Markets. Prices switch to local currency once Shopify Payments is on.
  • Languages: add and publish languages in Settings > Languages, then translate with the free Translate & Adapt app.
  • Theme texts: change any button or label in Themes > ... > Edit default theme content.

Questions

The Filters button does not show.

Install Shopify Search & Discovery and add at least one filter in the app.

The phone menu is empty.

Pick a menu in Header > Main links, or create a menu with the handle main-menu.

Prices do not switch currency.

Local currencies need Shopify Payments and at least two markets in Settings > Markets.

How do I update the theme?

When a new version is out, the Themes page shows an update. It installs as a new copy with your settings and content carried over; check it and publish when ready.

Something else?

Write to us on the support page. We answer within two business days.
